Apa itu Sinagri?What is Sinagri?

Sinagri (sebelumnya bernama Tumbuhi, sebelum itu AgriMRV) adalah sistem pemantauan lahan sawah berbasis citra satelit. Tujuannya sederhana: tahu jadwal tanam dan panen tiap petak sawah, tahu kondisi airnya (kering/tergenang), dan menghitung perkiraan pengurangan emisi karbon dari praktik pengelolaan air hemat (AWD — Alternate Wetting and Drying) — semua tanpa harus keliling sawah setiap hari.

Sinagri (formerly Tumbuhi, before that AgriMRV) is a satellite-based rice paddy monitoring system. The goal is simple: know each plot's planting/harvest schedule, know its water condition (dry/flooded), and estimate carbon emission reduction from water-saving irrigation practices (AWD — Alternate Wetting and Drying) — without needing to physically visit every field every day.

Analoginya: bayangkan Anda punya "mata di langit" (satelit Sentinel milik Uni Eropa, gratis dipakai siapa saja) yang lewat di atas sawah Anda tiap beberapa hari, memotret dalam beberapa "warna" yang mata manusia tidak bisa lihat — salah satunya bisa menembus awan dan "melihat" apakah tanah di bawah sedang basah atau kering, walau permukaannya sama-sama hijau dari atas.
Analogy: think of it as having "eyes in the sky" (Europe's free Sentinel satellites) passing over your fields every few days, capturing images in "colors" invisible to the human eye — one of which can see through clouds and detect whether the soil underneath is wet or dry, even when the surface looks the same shade of green from above.

Rumus & ReferensiFormulas & References

Setiap rumus di bawah dijelaskan pakai bahasa awam + sumber ilmiahnya, supaya bisa dirujuk langsung saat presentasi.

Every formula below is explained in plain language plus its scientific source, so it can be cited directly in presentations.

1. NDVI — Kesehatan TanamanPlant Health

NDVI = (NIR − Merah) / (NIR + Merah) = (B08 − B04) / (B08 + B04)
Daun yang sehat memantulkan banyak cahaya inframerah (tidak terlihat mata) tapi menyerap cahaya merah untuk fotosintesis. Makin besar selisihnya, makin sehat/rimbun tanamannya. Skalanya -1 sampai 1 — sawah sehat biasanya di angka 0.5–0.8.
Healthy leaves reflect a lot of infrared light (invisible to us) but absorb red light for photosynthesis. The bigger the gap, the healthier/denser the crop. Scale runs -1 to 1 — healthy paddy typically sits around 0.5–0.8.
Sumber: rumus standar Rouse et al. (1974), dipakai luas di remote sensing pertanian.

2. NDWI — Deteksi Genangan AirStanding Water Detection

NDWI = (Hijau − NIR) / (Hijau + NIR) = (B03 − B08) / (B03 + B08)
Air memantulkan cahaya hijau tapi menyerap hampir semua inframerah — kebalikan dari daun. Jadi kalau nilainya positif/tinggi, kemungkinan besar ada genangan air di petak itu; kalau negatif, tanahnya kering.
Water reflects green light but absorbs almost all infrared — the opposite of leaves. So a positive/high value likely means standing water in that plot; negative means dry soil.
Sumber: formula McFeeters (1996) — khusus untuk deteksi badan air, berbeda dari NDWI versi Gao (1996) yang untuk kelembapan vegetasi. Project ini sengaja pakai versi McFeeters karena tujuannya deteksi genangan, bukan stres kelembapan tanaman.

3. NBR — Deteksi Area TerbakarBurn Area Detection

NBR = (NIR − SWIR2) / (NIR + SWIR2) = (B08 − B12) / (B08 + B12)
Sumber: Normalized Burn Ratio standar, dipakai badan geologi AS (USGS) untuk pemetaan kebakaran.

4. SAR VV/VH — Radar Tembus AwanCloud-Penetrating Radar

flood_index = VH / VV  →  ≥0.28 = basah, ≤0.22 = kering, 0.22–0.28 = zona abu-abu
Radar mengirim gelombang sendiri (tidak bergantung matahari/awan) dan mengukur berapa banyak yang memantul balik dalam 2 arah polarisasi berbeda (VV dan VH). Rasio keduanya dipakai sebagai penanda basah/kering.
Radar sends its own signal (independent of sunlight/clouds) and measures how much bounces back in two different polarizations (VV and VH). The ratio is used as a wet/dry indicator.

⚠ Belum diverifikasi Studi ilmiah yang kami cek justru menunjukkan sinyal VH biasanya paling rendah saat lahan tergenang penuh — bukan rasio VH/VV yang naik. Arah formula ini kemungkinan sudah benar (threshold-nya sudah dikalibrasi dari data asli 08 Juli 2026), tapi belum dicocokkan ke tanggal yang benar-benar diketahui basah/kering di lapangan. Ini item terbuka di Roadmap. Scientific studies we checked show VH signal is typically lowest during full flooding — not a rising VH/VV ratio. This formula's direction is likely correct (thresholds were calibrated against real data on 2026-07-08), but hasn't been matched against dates with confirmed real-world wet/dry conditions. This is an open Roadmap item.

Sumber: Rice Crop Monitoring Using Sentinel-1 SAR Data (MDPI, 2022) — mdpi.com/2072-4292/14/14/3254

5. Estimasi CO2e — Perkiraan Pengurangan Emisi Karbon dari AWDEstimated Carbon Emission Reduction from AWD

CH4 gross (kg) = Hari Aerasi × 1.22 kg/ha/hari × Luas (ha)
CO2e NET (ton) = (CH4 gross × 27 × 0.653) / 1000
Kenapa rumus ini ada dua "gas" yang saling berlawanan? Sawah yang digenangi terus-menerus menghasilkan gas metana (CH4) dari bakteri di lumpur — kalau sengaja dikeringkan berkala (AWD), metananya berkurang jauh. Tapi ada efek samping: tanah yang beroksigen (kering) malah memicu gas lain, N2O, naik sedikit. N2O jauh lebih "jahat" untuk pemanasan global per kilogramnya (273x lipat CO2, dibanding metana yang "cuma" 27x) — jadi kalau cuma dihitung metananya turun tanpa dikurangi kenaikan N2O, hasilnya terlalu bagus dari kondisi sebenarnya (overstated). Rumus ini sudah memasukkan koreksi itu (angka 0.653) berdasarkan studi ilmiah lintas negara, bukan cuma menghitung sepihak.
Why does this formula involve two opposing gases? Continuously flooded paddies produce methane (CH4) from bacteria in the mud — periodic intentional drying (AWD) cuts that a lot. But there's a side effect: oxygenated (dry) soil triggers a different gas, N2O, to rise slightly. N2O is far more potent per kilogram for global warming (273x CO2, vs methane's "only" 27x) — so counting only the methane drop without netting out the N2O rise would overstate the real benefit. This formula already includes that correction (the 0.653 factor) based on cross-country scientific studies, not a one-sided count.

Honesty note: this is an illustrative formula, not a direct field measurement or an official carbon audit. The 0.653 correction factor is a general average, not calibrated specifically for Bali's soil/climate conditions. For any official claim to an auditor or carbon market, it must first be reviewed by a qualified GHG accountant.

6. NBR — Deteksi Pembakaran JeramiStraw Burning Detection

NBR = (NIR − SWIR2) / (NIR + SWIR2) = (B08 − B12) / (B08 + B12)  →  < -0.25 = terbakar berat
Tanah/vegetasi bekas terbakar menyerap gelombang inframerah-pendek (SWIR) jauh lebih banyak daripada lahan sehat — jadi nilai NBR anjlok drastis setelah kebakaran. Dipakai badan geologi AS (USGS) untuk memetakan kebakaran hutan.
Burned soil/vegetation absorbs far more short-wave infrared (SWIR) than healthy land — so NBR drops sharply after a fire. Used by the US Geological Survey (USGS) for wildfire mapping.

Chart tren NBR di aplikasi sudah menandai titik data ber-awan (cloud >15%) dengan warna merah + peringatan, persis pola yang sama dengan chart NDVI/NDWI — jadi tidak ada langkah tambahan yang perlu diperbaiki di sini. Checked 2026-07-18 for Budidaya BSO plot (237 data rows, Jul 2023–Jul 2026): 0 rows ever crossed the severe-burn threshold (-0.25) — the "no straw burning" claim holds up against available data. The app's NBR trend chart already flags cloud-contaminated points (cloud >15%) in red with a warning, matching the same pattern used for the NDVI/NDWI charts — so no further fix is needed here.

7. Bukti Intentionality AWDAWD Intentionality Evidence (Curah Hujan vs Kondisi Lahan)(Rainfall vs Field Condition)

Pertanyaan yang mau dijawab: kalau satelit mendeteksi lahan "kering", apakah itu karena petani SENGAJA mengeringkan (praktik AWD asli, yang jadi dasar klaim karbon), atau cuma kebetulan lagi musim kemarau/tidak hujan (kekeringan alami, tidak ada nilai tambah untuk klaim karbon)? Sebelum ada data cuaca, ini TIDAK BISA dijawab — cuma tahu "kering", tidak tahu kenapa.

Question this answers: when the satellite detects a "dry" field, is that because the farmer INTENTIONALLY drained it (real AWD practice, the basis for a carbon claim), or just coincidentally in a dry season/no rain (natural drought, no carbon-claim value)? Before weather data existed, this COULD NOT be answered — we only knew "dry," not why.

UNTUK SETIAP titik lahan berstatus "kering":
  hujan_5hari = SUM(curah hujan, 5 hari sebelum tanggal itu)
  JIKA hujan_5hari >= 5mm → hitung sebagai "bukti pengeringan aktif"
awd_intentionality_pct = (jumlah "bukti aktif") / (total titik kering) × 100
Analoginya: bayangkan tetangga Anda halamannya kering. Kalau seminggu terakhir memang tidak hujan sama sekali, wajar halamannya kering — tidak ada yang istimewa. Tapi kalau BARU SAJA hujan deras 3 hari lalu, dan halamannya SUDAH kering lagi sekarang — itu tandanya ada yang SENGAJA menguras airnya (bukan menguap sendiri secepat itu). Logika yang sama dipakai di sini: kalau lahan sawah "kering" versi satelit itu terjadi PADAHAL baru hujan lebat, itu tanda kuat petani sengaja membuang airnya (AWD aktif) — air hujan yang jatuh tidak mungkin hilang secepat itu secara alami.
Analogy: imagine your neighbor's yard is dry. If it truly hasn't rained all week, that's unremarkable. But if it rained heavily just 3 days ago and the yard is ALREADY dry again — that signals someone deliberately drained the water (it can't evaporate that fast on its own). Same logic here: if a satellite-"dry" paddy field occurs despite recent heavy rain, that's strong evidence the farmer intentionally drained it (active AWD) — rainwater doesn't naturally vanish that quickly.

All 6 seasons score above 40% — a consistent signal that field drying in this project isn't a weather coincidence. But there's one important caveat about the 100% figure (wayan suparta, Nov-Mar): that season fell during peak rainy season (per 2026-07-08 data: 88.9% of optical scans failed due to heavy cloud) — if it rains ALMOST EVERY DAY, ANY dry point will automatically "coincidentally" have rain in the prior 5 days, nothing special about it. So 100% during rainy season is less meaningful than, say, 68% during dry season (Budidaya BSO, Mar-Jul) — in dry season, rain is rarer, so a field staying dry despite rain is actually STRONGER evidence. This metric doesn't yet account for "how often it rains in general" as a baseline comparison — a future improvement idea, not a claim of perfection today.

For any official claim/audit, direct field confirmation with the farmer/pekaseh is still required.

8. Kenapa Interval Scan 6 Hari?Why a 6-Day Scan Interval?

Alasannya: interval 6 hari (mode intensif, otomatis aktif pas ada musim tanam berjalan) dipilih karena itu adalah siklus ulang fisik satelit Sentinel-1 (radar, sumber data utama untuk deteksi genangan air tembus awan) — sudah mendekati batas maksimal kecepatan satelit itu sendiri lewat lagi ke lokasi yang sama. Minta data lebih sering dari itu percuma: bukan soal hemat kuota saja, tapi secara fisik belum ada data baru untuk diambil — satelitnya belum lewat lagi.
The reason: the 6-day interval (intensive mode, auto-activated during an active planting season) was chosen because that's the physical repeat cycle of the Sentinel-1 satellite (radar, the primary data source for cloud-penetrating water detection) — already close to the satellite's own maximum revisit speed over the same location. Requesting data more often than that is pointless: it's not just about saving quota, there's physically no new data to fetch yet — the satellite hasn't passed over again.
SatelitSatelliteDipakai untukUsed forSiklus ulang fisikPhysical repeat cycle
Sentinel-1 (radar)SAR VV/VH — deteksi genangan tembus awan6 hari
Sentinel-2 (optik)NDVI/NDWI/NBR5 hari

Sentinel-2 sebenarnya sedikit lebih cepat (5 hari), tapi sistem ini menyatukan ke 1 angka (6 hari) untuk kesederhanaan — 1 interval dikelola, bukan 2 interval berbeda untuk optik dan radar. Ini "mengorbankan" 1 hari potensi data optik ekstra demi kesederhanaan sistem, bukan kelalaian.

Sentinel-2 is actually slightly faster (5 days), but this system unifies to 1 number (6 days) for simplicity — 1 interval to manage, not 2 different intervals for optical vs radar. This "sacrifices" 1 day of potential extra optical data for the sake of system simplicity, not an oversight.

Implikasi: kalau ditanya auditor "kenapa bukan interval lebih rapat", jawabannya bukan "biar hemat kuota" saja — itu batas fisik satelit itu sendiri. Menambah kepastian data ke depan bukan soal mempercepat interval (sudah mentok), tapi lewat ground-truthing (piezometer) yang independen dari satelit.

Implication: if an auditor asks "why not a tighter interval", the answer isn't just "to save quota" — it's the satellite's own physical limit. Adding more data certainty going forward isn't about a faster interval (already maxed out) but through ground-truthing (piezometer) that's independent of the satellite.

Sumber: ESA — Sentinel-1 Satellite Constellation; Sentinel-2 revisit time. Dicek 19 Juli 2026.
